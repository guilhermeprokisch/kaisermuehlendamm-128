Kaisermühlendamm 128: what our building costs

Every flat gets the same annual operating-cost statement (Betriebskostenabrechnung) from ÖRAG. The building totals are the same for all of us; only our share changes with the flat size. This page compares 2024 and 2025.

A residents' initiative, not an official ÖRAG page. Amounts are net (without VAT) unless marked. Updated 16.10.2026.

The building

  • Am Kaisermühlendamm 128 / Kaisermühlenstraße 8, 1220 Wien, finished in 2023, 2 buildings.
  • 251 flats, 35 small flats for temporary living, 135 garage spaces, 2 shops. About 13,900 m².
  • Owner: EFF-PL Immobilien Verwaltungs GmbH, part of the REAL INVEST Austria fund of Bank Austria Real Invest (UniCredit). Fund page with photos
  • Property manager: ÖRAG (Hausverwaltung), immobilienverwaltung@oerag.at, +43 1 5324440-0.

Summary

  • General costs 2025: €354,556, +24.7% on 2024 (€284,282).
  • That is €2.13 per m² per month, or €2.34 with 10% VAT.
  • Largest increase: service & maintenance, +83% (+€46,008). The biggest part of it is ista, €37,907 for the meters.
  • Water and sewage: €6,929 for the whole building in 2024, €126,650 in 2025. That is not a price rise. 2024 was barely billed (one flat paid €32 for the whole year); 2025 is about one year of water at Vienna's tariff.

What stands out

ista alone is €37,907, more than a third of all maintenance, for maintaining and renting the meters. ista also bills €13,014 for the annual billing inside the heating costs, so about €51,000 a year goes to ista.

Maintenance rose by €46,008 from 2024 to 2025. The 2024 long statement is not in the portal, so we cannot yet say which contracts are new. ista's €37,907 would explain most of the jump.

Where to find it: ÖRAG portal → Dokumente → Gebäude → 15/22128_M_Am Kaisermühlendamm 128 → Objektakt → Abrechnung → BK Abrechnung → 20260709-ABR-53132471.pdf. The 2023 one (December only) is there too; the 2024 one is missing.

Heating and water (billed by your meters)

Item20242025Change

Each flat pays by its own ista meters.

Why water jumped from €6,929 to €126,650. For a building of this size, €6,929 cannot be a year of water: in 2024 the water was barely billed. One flat (two people) paid €32 for all of 2024 and €510 for 2025. The 2025 amount is about one year's use at Vienna's tariff, roughly €4.80 per m³ including VAT. So 2025 is the normal level, and 2024 was the exception. ÖRAG has been asked about the water statement.

What a cubic metre costs. These are estimates from one flat, dividing its 2024 and 2025 bills by its ista meter readings, VAT included:

  • Cold water and sewage: about €4.80 per m³.
  • Heating the hot water: about €10.10 per m³, so a cubic metre of hot water costs about €15 in total.
  • Heating: about €0.16 per kWh.

An 8-minute shower (about 70 litres, two thirds hot) costs roughly €0.80.

The building is heated by a heat pump. Its electricity cost €114,478 in 2025 (Wien Energie, plus €13,014 for ista's billing). In summer it runs at about €6,400 a month for hot water alone; in winter it rises to €10,000–13,600. So heating itself costs roughly €38,000 a year, and hot water the rest.
