Kaisermühlendamm 128: too warm in the flats

Many flats in our building are very warm this heating season, even with the room thermostat (the Roth dial, 1–6) turned low. A neighbour started this to find out whether it is the whole building and to ask ÖRAG to check the heating system.

A residents' initiative, not an official ÖRAG page. Updated 13.10.2026.

Temperature poll (58 flats, about 20% of all units)

AnswerFlatsShare
  • 71% are over 25 °C or find it too warm.
  • 88% are at 23 °C or more, or find it too warm.
  • Nobody said the temperature is fine.

What the standards say

  • Heating systems are designed for 20 °C in living rooms (ÖNORM EN 12831).
  • The European comfort standard EN 16798-1 accepts 20–25 °C in winter.
  • For bedrooms, 18–20 °C is the usual advice.

Reported to ÖRAG

Service request 182177, since 08.10.2026. The poll results were added on 11.10.2026. On 13.10.2026 ÖRAG answered that they have sent their specialist company to check it. We asked that the check covers the central heating settings, not only single flats.

The building was finished in 2023. Under Austrian law the warranty for defects in buildings is 3 years (§ 933 ABGB), so if the cause turns out to be in the construction, it should be raised within that period.

How to help

The more flats report it, the sooner ÖRAG has the system checked. Open oerag.idwell.com/requests → "Servicemeldung erstellen" → Mängel → Einheit → Heizung / Klima / Lüftung → Fußbodenheizung, and paste this. Keep it short; the portal rejects long descriptions.

Titel: Wohnung zu warm, Heizung prüfen

Beschreibung: Meine Wohnung (Top [Nr.]) hat ca. [XX] °C, auch nachts, obwohl der Raumthermostat niedrig eingestellt ist. Bitte die Heizungsanlage des Gebäudes prüfen, siehe auch Servicemeldung 182177.

No portal account? Send it to immobilienverwaltung@oerag.at.
